## Plan: 16 weeks to raise FTP and speed

Goal: raise FTP from the current value to the goal in the profile (+10–12 % per cycle is realistic), develop speed, cadence 85–95 rpm. A 20-minute FTP test every 4 weeks (result × 0.95), then recalculate zones.

Zones from FTP: Z1 < 60 %, Z2 60–80 %, Z3 81–90 %, Z4 91–105 %, Z5 106–120 %, Z6 > 120 %.

**Phase 1, weeks 1–4 — aerobic base.** Volume from 2 up to 5–6 h/week, cadence drills, nothing above Z3. Typical week: Z2 75–90 min at 85–92 rpm; sweet spot 3×15 or 2×20 min at 88–93 %; long Z2 90–120 min; 45 min recovery ride. Week 4: recovery and test.

**Phase 2, weeks 5–8 — threshold.** 6–7 h/week. Sweet spot 3×15 → 2×25 min, threshold 4×8 → 2×20 min at 100–105 %, sprints 6–10×10 s all-out with full recovery, long Z2 up to 140 min. Week 8: recovery and test.

**Phase 3, weeks 9–12 — VO2max.** 7–8 h/week. VO2max 5×3 → 6×3 → 5×4 min at 115 %, threshold 2×20 or 3×12 min, threshold + sprint combo, long Z2+ with 30 min in Z3, up to 160 min. Week 12: recovery and test.

**Phase 4, weeks 13–16 — speed and peak.** 5.5–6.5 h/week. 8×1 min at 130 %, VO2max with sprints, threshold 2×20, group rides or races. Week 16: taper and final test.

For athletes over 40: 48 hours between hard sessions, 7.5–8 h of sleep, 30–40 g of protein after hard sessions.
